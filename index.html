Automated carbon fiber filament winder

An automated winder that produces carbon fiber composite tubes 3 ft long, with a 3 in inner diameter and 4 mm walls. A custom six-motor motion system controls mandrel rotation, carriage travel, and fiber delivery and angle.

  • Six-motor motion system
  • Carbon fiber composites
  • Onshape CAD
  • Open-source publication
Technical details
  • Motion system: coordinates mandrel rotation speed, carriage travel along the tube, resin bath positioning, and fiber angle.
  • CAD: fully modeled and simulated in Onshape before machining and assembly.
  • Open source: design files, motor controllers, and software logic are being documented for academic publication.

VEX Robotics Competition. Judges Award recipient.

VEX V5 competition robot

I designed, programmed, and refined the mechanical assemblies for a competition robot on the VEX V5 platform. I focused on gear-drive ratios, intake mechanisms, and frame rigidity under match loads.

  • Judges Award
  • VEX V5
  • Mechanical kinematics
  • Competitive drivetrain
Technical details
  • Tuned the gear train and motor distribution for maneuverability and defensive stability.
